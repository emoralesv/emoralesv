# Hi, I'm Eduardo Morales Vargas 👋

### AI/ML Engineer · PhD in Computer Science

I develop custom machine learning models and intelligent systems, combining research with practical implementation. My work covers computer vision, signal processing, robotics, and the integration of AI into software and physical systems.

I enjoy working across the full development process: understanding the problem, preparing datasets, designing and evaluating models, and integrating them into applications. My experience spans agriculture, manufacturing, satellite communications, and biomedical signal analysis.

## 🔬 Areas of Experience

### Computer Vision & Robotics
I have developed models for object detection, segmentation, classification, tracking, and multiview analysis. My work includes agricultural perception and automated harvesting, integrating model outputs with ROS so robotic systems can act on visual information.

I also work with edge inference, using NVIDIA Jetson and TensorRT to bring computer vision models into systems with hardware and latency constraints.

### Signal Processing & Satellite Communications
My experience includes biomedical signals, time series, and radio-frequency IQ data. I have worked on satellite signal analysis, modulation recognition, and data recovery pipelines using software-defined radio.

This work connects signal processing, machine learning, and distributed acquisition systems.

### Intelligent Systems & Simulation
My research interests include digital twins, simulation, and learning methods for robotic tasks. I am interested in how data-driven models can represent physical processes and support perception, prediction, and decision-making.

### LLM Applications & Automation
I am expanding my work into LLM-powered applications, retrieval-augmented generation, agents, and tool calling. I am particularly interested in connecting language models with custom APIs and technical tools to support useful workflows.
